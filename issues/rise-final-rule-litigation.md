The Department of Education's Reimagining and Improving Student Education
(RISE) Final Rule narrowed which graduate programs count as "professional
degree" programs for federal loan-limit purposes under the One Big Beautiful
Bill Act (OBBBA). Three separate lawsuits challenge the definition. Opened
from horizon-90.md's 2026-09-28 scan.
