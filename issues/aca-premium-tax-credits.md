## Virginia impact

- More than 400,000 Virginians buy coverage on the state exchange; over 300,000
  used the lapsed credits (SCC data via Virginia Mercury, 2026-01-15,
  <https://virginiamercury.com/2026/01/15/virginia-congressman-wittman-among-just-17-republicans-nationally-to-back-aca-credits-extension/>). verified 2026-09-26
- The earlier "411,000" and "more than 20 percent" figures had no source URL
  recorded. Do not reuse them until sourced
- Virginia's Insurance Marketplace open enrollment: November 1 to January 29;
  December 15 for coverage starting January 1
  (<https://help.marketplace.virginia.gov/hc/en-us/articles/11972839569293-What-is-Open-Enrollment>). verified 2026-09-26.
  Virginia runs its own exchange, so HealthCare.gov dates do not apply

The November 1 date is the forcing function: the Senate sits September 14 to
October 2 and then leaves for the election, so that stretch is the last floor
time before Virginians see 2027 prices.
