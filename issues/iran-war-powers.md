resolutions keep failing in the Senate, so the live lever is the money: a
White House supplemental request and a House-adopted reconciliation plan.
Indivisible's national campaign is "Stop Trump's Disastrous War on Iran."

## Corrections and traps

- A war powers **concurrent** resolution is not binding and never reaches the
  President. Do not describe a Senate passage as ending the war.
- The $73 billion (reconciliation plan) and $67.1 billion (supplemental
  request) are two different vehicles. Name which one an ask is about.
- The House vote was party-line in Virginia. There is no movable House member
  on the war powers question; leverage is on the money and on public answers.
