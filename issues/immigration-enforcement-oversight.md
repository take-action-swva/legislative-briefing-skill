# Immigration enforcement funding and oversight

**Slug:** `immigration-enforcement-oversight`
**Opened:** 2026-09-02
**Last touched:** 2026-09-02
**Status:** active

Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) is funded through reconciliation
rather than annual appropriations, which removes the usual lever of
conditioning money on conduct. Oversight, siting decisions, and public
commitments are what remain.

Campaigns: Dismantling Detention, Immigrant Justice Summer.

---

| Bill / docket | What it is | Status | verified |
|---|---|---|---|
| H.R. 5007 (WATCH ICE Act) | Subramanyam bill requiring ICE to publicly report arrests, detentions, deportations | Referred to House Judiciary | 2026-09-02 |
| 287(g) Cooperation Act of 2026 | Cline bill conditioning Community Oriented Policing Services grants on local agencies signing ICE agreements. Kiggans original cosponsor | Introduced | 2026-09-02 |
| FY2026 Department of Homeland Security (DHS) law | Left ICE and Border Security Operations accounts at zero | Enacted after a department shutdown 2026-02-14 to 2026-04-30; agencies funded through reconciliation instead, **money running to 2029** | 2026-09-02 |
| FY2027 DHS appropriations | Roughly $28 billion more for ICE and Customs and Border Protection (CBP) | Approved by House appropriators, per the Brennan Center | 2026-09-02 |

**Cosponsors for H.R. 5007 were not retrieved** during the September research.
Run `./scripts/fetch-cosponsors.sh 119 hr 5007 VA` before any cosponsorship
ask — the script also separates withdrawn cosponsors, which the raw
congress.gov endpoint mixes in with current ones.

The $28 billion figure is from the Brennan Center, **an advocacy
organization**. Usable for context under Accuracy Rule 4, labeled as such.

## Corrections and traps

- **Do not build an ask around withholding annual appropriations from ICE.**
  That lever does not exist. The reconciliation money runs to 2029.
- **Immigrant Justice Summer has ended.** Any ask built on registering for a
  call is out of date. The materials and the money are still live.
- **Virginia's district lines did not change.** The mid-decade redistricting
  amendment was struck down by the Supreme Court of Virginia 2026-05-08 and
  the U.S. Supreme Court declined the emergency appeal 2026-05-15. The 2021
  maps are in force.
- **Wittman is Tier 3 here and Tier 1 on** [[aca-premium-tax-credits]], where
  he voted for the extension as one of 17 Republicans. Tier is per issue.
- The three core Dismantling Detention demands are local-government asks. Do
  not address them to members of Congress.
