## Corrections and traps

- This is a **defense bill** carrying election law, not an election bill. Asks
  that name the NDAA in general get a defense answer. Every ask must name the
  election provisions specifically.
- The two Senate cloture failures (50–46, 52–46) are the evidence base. Do not
  describe the Senate as having "passed" or "rejected" the policy outright.
- **Corrected 2026-09-02: the Senate SAVE Act is S. 128, not S. 1383.**
  S. 1383 in the 119th Congress is the Veterans Accessibility Advisory
  Committee Act, sponsored by Sen. Rick Scott, and is unrelated. The error came
  from the example in `skills/brief-full.md` Step 1 and was inherited here.
  Verified against congress.gov.
- **H.R. 22 is dormant, and briefing it as live misdirects organizing.** It has
  not moved since 2025-04-10. Asks that name H.R. 22 get a true and useless
  answer. The policy moved to the defense authorization.
- Always re-run `./scripts/fetch-cosponsors.sh` rather than reusing an older
  list. The endpoint mixes withdrawn cosponsors in with current ones.
