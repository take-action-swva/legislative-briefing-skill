# OMB Regulation for Federal Financial Assistance (the federal grants rule)

**Slug:** `federal-grants-rule`
**Opened:** 2026-09-02
**Last touched:** 2026-09-02
**Status:** active

An Office of Management and Budget (OMB) rule restructuring how roughly $1.2
trillion a year in federal grants is awarded and terminated. Not legislation —
this is a rulemaking, and congress.gov will not show it.

## What the rule does

- Routes awards through pre-review by political appointees
- Widens the risk factors agencies weigh to include an applicant's affiliations
- Standardizes authority to terminate a grant when agency priorities change

Virginia exposure: school divisions, health districts, universities, water
systems, volunteer fire departments, and small nonprofits.

## Corrections and traps

- **The block expires mid-fight, not after it.** December 11 is inside the
  appropriations endgame, not past it. Framing the rule as "blocked until
  December" understates the risk.
- This is a rulemaking. Do not look for it on congress.gov, and do not
  describe comment closure as though it were a vote.
- Linked to [[federal-funding-fy2027]]: whether the block survives depends on
  the full-year vehicle.
