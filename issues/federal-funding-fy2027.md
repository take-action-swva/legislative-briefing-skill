# Federal funding and the FY2027 appropriations fight

**Slug:** `federal-funding-fy2027`
**Opened:** 2026-09-02
**Last touched:** 2026-09-26
**Status:** active

Funding is held at fiscal year 2026 levels by a continuing resolution through
December 11, 2026. The twelve fiscal year 2027 bills are the live fight, and
they land in the four weeks between the election and that date.

## Corrections and traps

- The December 11 date is the continuing resolution's expiry, not an
  appropriations deadline in statute. It moves if a new CR passes.
- The October 1 shutdown risk is resolved. Do not carry forward urgency
  language built on it.
- **Chamber calendars differ.** The House sits Sep 1–3, 14–17, 22–25, 28–30
  and Oct 1. The Senate returns Sep 14 and sits through Oct 2. The September
  2026 digest's framing line said "both chambers leave October 2," which
  understated House floor time by about two weeks. See `calendar-119.md`.
