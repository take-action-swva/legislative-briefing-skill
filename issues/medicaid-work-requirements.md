New federal Medicaid work and community engagement requirements, created by
the 2025 reconciliation law (One Big Beautiful Bill Act), take effect
January 1, 2027 under a Centers for Medicare and Medicaid Services (CMS)
interim final rule. Some Virginia-specific changes begin as early as October
2026. Opened from horizon-90.md's 2026-09-28 scan.

## Corrections and traps

- The January 1, 2027 date is fixed by the interim final rule's own terms,
  not by anyone's stated intent — do not soften it to Expected or Watch.
- Some requirements phase in earlier (October 2026) for some enrollees per
  VCU Health; do not state a single uniform date for all Virginia Medicaid
  expansion enrollees.
