## Virginia Political Data & News

| Source | URL | Best For | Reliability | Last Verified |
|--------|-----|----------|-------------|---------------|
| VPAP | vpap.org | Virginia political data, district context, donor maps, redistricting tracking | high | 2026-06-01 |
| Virginia Independent | virginiaindependentnews.com | Virginia-focused political news and analysis | moderate | 2026-09-28 |
| Blue Virginia | bluevirginia.us | Virginia progressive political coverage | moderate | 2026-06-01 |

**Redistricting note:** Virginia redistricting was actively litigated in early
2026. Always verify current district boundaries and member assignments at
vpap.org before using district numbers in constituent outreach. Do not rely
on memory or prior briefings.

**Virginia Independent, downgraded 2026-09-28:** it is a project of American
Independent Media, a 501(c)(4) that operates similarly named outlets in
several states. That is an advocacy funding structure, not the independent
or nonpartisan ownership "high" implies here — even though it publishes real,
bylined, dated reporting. Verify key facts elsewhere before citing.
